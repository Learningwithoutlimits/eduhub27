Workshop booklet

Fill in the keynote, the structure of the day, the four workshop rounds with their times, and twelve workshops from the workshop booklet, with speaker photos. Nothing is saved until you check the details and press Save.

Keynote

Shown on the EduHub page. Everyone attends, so staff do not book it.

Structure of the day

Shown as a timeline at the top of the EduHub page and beside the booking form. Until you save your own, the structure of the day from the booklet is used. Untick groups for lines that apply to some staff only. A workshop line linked to a round shows each person the workshop they chose for it.

Workshop rounds

Each member of staff is offered only the rounds their group attends, decided by the phase of the school they choose when booking. They choose one workshop in each of those rounds and cannot choose the same workshop twice.

Workshops

Each workshop runs in every round unless you untick some. Places are per round. The recommended audience is a guide only, and anyone can book any workshop.

Schools and teams

Staff choose their school or team from this list when they book. Its phase decides which workshop rounds they are offered. If the list is empty, staff type their school in and are offered every round.
